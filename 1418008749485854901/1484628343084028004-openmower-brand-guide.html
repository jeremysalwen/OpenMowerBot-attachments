03 — Logo Usage

Variations

Use the appropriate logo version based on the background. Never modify the proportions, colors, or spacing within the logo.

White — on dark background
White — on brand green
Dark — on white / light background
Icon — standalone / avatar
Do

Use the white version on any background darker than 50% value. Maintain clear space of at least ½× the logo height on all sides.

Don't

Never place the logo on a busy photograph, patterned background, or any color that reduces contrast below AA accessibility standards.

Do

Scale proportionally. Minimum display width is 120px for the full wordmark, 32px for the standalone icon.

Don't

Never distort, rotate, recolor, add effects (shadows, outlines, gradients), or rearrange the icon and wordmark relationship.
